The Four Locks

No one should be permitted to govern from safety.

🔒 Lock 1: Residential Mandate

Officials and their families must live in their constituency, using public schools and hospitals.

🔒 Lock 2: Public Accounts Transparency

Personal account balances of officials must be visible to the public in real-time, updated monthly.

🔒 Lock 3: Exit Prohibition

No official may relocate permanently outside the country for 10 years after their term ends.

🔒 Lock 4: Development Bonus

Financial bonuses are awarded only for measurable, independently verified improvements in the constituency.

Built for consequence, not comfort.
